## Description (full body — paste into the itch.io description editor)

**Choose your civilization. Claim the galaxy.**

GALACTIC ZERO is a sci-fi strategic card game where two sides battle for control of a 5×7 grid. Draft a hand of faction cards, place them into contested rows and columns, and out-maneuver your opponent through power edge battles and special abilities.

**11 Playable Civilizations**
Each faction has its own card deck, lore, and visual identity — from the organic horror of The Brood to the crystalline precision of Crystallis, the entropic decay of Entropy, the ethereal Veil, and beyond.

**Card Abilities**
Cards carry unique special powers that trigger on placement — Pierce through equal edges, Flank for an extra turn, Surge to empower adjacent allies, LAMB to slip past zone restrictions, and a dozen more. No two factions play alike.

**Three Ways to Play**
- **Solo** — Challenge the AI across 3 difficulty settings (Passive / Balanced / Aggressive)
- **Local** — Pass-and-play matches on one screen
- **Multiplayer** — Real-time PvP via room code. Share a link, your opponent joins instantly

**Leaderboard**
Top scores are tracked globally. Win fast, win big, climb the board.

---

This game runs entirely in the browser — no download, no install. Just pick a faction and play.

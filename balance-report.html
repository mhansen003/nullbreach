Methodology Notes

Simulation: Python greedy AI plays all 110 ordered faction pairs (8 games each = 880 games per iteration). VP scoring uses pure delta: winner rows/cols - loser rows/cols. Card edges are flipped N/S when deck plays as AI (top side).

Key Finding: Win rate is primarily driven by the faction's average (N+S)/2 value (determines performance as both player and AI). Factions with low S (Terran S=2.7, Brood S=2.7, Choir S=3.4) were structurally weak as AI until S edges were boosted. Gas's problem was pure E/W weakness (E=2-3) causing systematic column losses.

Structural Limitation: High N/low S factions (Crystallis, Lithos, Veil) maintain identity tradeoffs. 3 factions remain slightly below range (34-39%) due to inherent asymmetry and simulation variance at 880-game sample size.

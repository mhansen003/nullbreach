② FULL ABILITY TAXONOMY — ALL TYPES
PLACEMENT
Controls WHERE and WHEN a card can be placed
RUSH LIVE
Place adjacent to any enemy, bypass tier zones. Only restriction: cannot enter enemy home row.
Best for: aggressive factions, swarm decks
FLANK LIVE
After placing, take an immediate extra turn before the AI responds.
Best for: tempo factions, mid-game momentum
PHANTOM NEW IDEA
Can be placed anywhere in own home 2 rows — ignores zone expansion rules entirely. Flexible anchor.
Best for: evasive/light factions (Veil?)
AMBUSH NEW IDEA
On placement, ALL adjacent enemy cards each permanently lose -1 from all 4 of their edges. Area debuff — rewards clustering near multiple enemies.
Best for: assassin/aggressive factions (Void?). Strong mid-game when enemies are packed.
SNIPER NEW IDEA
When placed, cancels out the topmost enemy card in this card's column — that enemy contributes 0 VP for its row until end of game. Targeted column control.
Best for: long-range/precision factions. Devastating in columns the enemy is winning.
HAT TRICK NEW IDEA
When placed as the middle card of 3 friendly cards in a row (any direction), this card's facing-edge value toward each flanking friendly card is added evenly across that friendly card's other 3 edges. Rewards tight formation play — your flankers get stronger the more connected the line is.
Best for: formation-based factions (Choir? Terran?). Requires setup but turns a 3-card line into a powerhouse.
BIRTHRIGHT NEW IDEA
At game start, if this card is in your deck, a random 16th bonus card (tier between T2 and T3) is spawned and added to your hand. You start with one more card than normal — a structural deck advantage.
Best for: factions with strong mid-tier cards. Creates early-game flexibility and density advantage.
COMBAT MODIFIERS
Changes HOW edge comparisons resolve
DOUBLE STRIKE LIVE
Fires a second comparison at half-strength after the primary. Can flip near-ties into wins.
Best for: offensive factions, high edge-value decks
PIERCE LIVE
Ties in edge comparisons count as wins for this card. Eliminates draw outcomes.
Best for: balanced-edge factions (Quantum?)
SHIELD LIVE
Absorbs the first edge-comparison loss. One-time defensive barrier per card.
Best for: defensive anchors, T1 home row holders
MIRROR NEW IDEA
When this card is sandwiched between 2 enemy cards in the same direction (both H or both V), the attack power values of those two enemies are swapped/reversed — their facing edges get exchanged, weakening the stronger attacker.
Best for: counter-play factions (Crystallis?). Punishes enemies who cluster in a line.
OVERWHELM NEW IDEA
If this card wins by 3+, it wins BOTH the row AND column for that cell simultaneously (double-line claim).
Best for: high-power flagship/T4 cards
AURA / BUFF
Affects adjacent cards on or after placement
COMMANDER LIVE
Adjacent same-tier friendly cards get +2 all edges on placement. Tier-gated buff.
Best for: structured/military factions (Terran)
SPAWN LIVE
Adjacent same-tier BROOD (faction-gated) cards get +2 all edges. Faction-identity version of COMMANDER.
Brood-exclusive. Encourage mono-faction hive play.
BOOST LIVE
Adjacent friendly cards (any tier) get +1 all edges. Wider but shallower than COMMANDER.
Best for: support roles, swarm factions
SWEEP LIVE
On placement, this card's edges all normalize to its 2nd-highest value. Spreads power evenly.
Best for: cards with extreme spike-low profiles
INTIMIDATE NEW IDEA
On placement, adjacent enemy cards each lose -1 from their single highest edge permanently. Targeted precision debuff.
Best for: aggressive/dominance factions (Void?). Shaves the enemy's sharpest weapon.
SCORING / VICTORY
Changes how VP is calculated or won
PIERCE LIVE
Tie = win for this card (affects line-win calculation). Removes draws from equation.
Strongest in balanced-edge decks
DENSITY NEW IDEA
This card counts as 1.5× its power when calculating VP for its row/col (rounded up). Scoring multiplier.
Best for: heavy flagship cards
ECHO NEW IDEA
If this card's row AND col are both won, scores double VP for this card only. Corner-play reward.
Best for: factions that reward positioning (Quantum, Choir)
